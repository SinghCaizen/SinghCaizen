Our Businesses

Four divisions, one standard

The Singh Caizen group is being built across four focus areas. Each division will be announced in detail as it launches — and each will be held to the same standard: quality, honesty and improvement, every single day.

Division 01

Infrastructure & Projects

Project execution with a focus on quality, safety and timelines — the foundations on which neighbourhoods, businesses and cities grow.

Focus areasCivil & infrastructure projects · End-to-end project delivery · Quality & safety systems

Division 02

Technology & Services

Digital and professional services for modern businesses — practical technology, honestly delivered, that helps our clients work faster and serve better.

Focus areasDigital products & platforms · IT & business services · Support & consulting

Division 03

Consumer & Trade

Everyday products and trade, built on trust — because a group earns its name at the counter, in the market and in the home, one customer at a time.

Focus areasRetail & distribution · Consumer brands · Customer experience

Division 04

Singh Caizen Tower

The group’s own company for landmark towers and spaces — buildings that carry the Singh Caizen name, built and run to the group’s one standard.

StatusCompany announced · Full profile to follow
