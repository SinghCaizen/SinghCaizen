How We Grow

Announced only when ready

A division of this group is not launched with a press release and a promise. It follows an order — and the order is not changed for anyone.

Formed with care

Every division begins as a discipline before it becomes a business: its standards, its leadership and its governance are settled first.

Introduced in full

When a division is ready, its complete profile is published here — what it does, who leads it and how to reach it. Nothing less counts as an announcement.

Held to one standard

From its first day of work, each business is measured the same way as every other: quality, honesty and improvement, every single day.
