Singh Caizen Trusts is being established as the philanthropic arm of the Singh Caizen group — to work in health, education, livelihoods, water and the environment, beginning where the need is greatest.

Focus Areas

Where the Trust will work.

Five areas, chosen because they decide whether a family can move forward — health, learning, income, water and the land itself. Programmes in each area are being designed now.

Health

Primary care, prevention and health awareness for communities that travel farthest for the simplest treatment.

Programme design under way

Education

Learning support for children and young people — reading, schooling continuity and pathways to further study.

Programme design under way

Livelihoods

Skills, tools and market access that help farming and working families earn more from the work they already do.

Programme design under way

Water & Sanitation

Clean drinking water and basic sanitation — the two interventions that quietly change everything else.

Programme design under way

Environment

Tree cover, soil and water bodies — protecting the natural base that rural life depends on.

Programme design under way

Our Approach

Three commitments, made before the first programme.

01

Begin at the ground

Work starts in the village and the ward — with the people the programme is for, not above them.

02

Stay for the long term

The Trust will enter a place to remain useful there — measured in years, not in visits.

03

Account for every rupee

Audited accounts and published reports, from the first year onward. What is given for society will be shown to society.
