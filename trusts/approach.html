Our Approach

How the Trust will work.

Four working rules that will govern every programme — set down now, before the first rupee is spent.

1 · Partner first

In most places, someone is already doing the work — a local organisation, a health worker, a teacher collective. The Trust's first instinct will be to fund and strengthen them, and to implement directly only where no capable hand exists.

2 · Design with, not for

Programmes will be designed with the community they serve — its panchayat, its women, its young people — before launch, not explained to them after it. If the ground says a design is wrong, the design changes.

3 · Stay and measure

The Trust will commit to its geographies for years and measure outcomes honestly — including publishing what did not work. A programme that cannot show its results will be redesigned or closed, not defended.

4 · Account in public

Independently audited accounts and an annual report will be published on this website every year, from the first year of operations. Donors, partners and the public will see the same numbers the trustees see.

Funding

Funded by the group, run for the public.

The Trust will be funded by contributions from the Singh Caizen group and its companies as they are established, and — in time — by others who wish to give alongside. Its accounts will remain separate, audited and published, whatever the source of funds.
