Focus Areas

Five areas. One reason each.

Each area was chosen for the same reason — it decides whether a family can move forward. Programmes in all five are being designed; what follows is the intent each programme will be built on.

Area 01

Health

For many rural families, the nearest dependable care is a day's travel away — so small illnesses become large ones. The Trust's health programmes will focus on the first mile: prevention, screening and primary care that reaches people where they live.

  • Preventive health camps and screening in underserved areas
  • Mother and child health — nutrition, immunisation awareness, safe practices
  • Support for local health workers and frontline facilities

Area 02

Education

Schooling breaks quietly — a child falls behind in reading, then leaves altogether. The Trust's education programmes will work on continuity: keeping children learning, and helping young people move from school to further study or skilled work.

  • Reading and foundational learning support for young children
  • Scholarships and guidance for students continuing beyond school
  • Support for community libraries and learning spaces

Area 03

Livelihoods

Most families the Trust will serve already work hard — on farms, in trades, in small shops. What they lack is rarely effort; it is skills, tools and a fair route to market. Livelihood programmes will be built around exactly those three gaps.

  • Skill training linked to real local demand
  • Support for farmer groups — better inputs, storage and market access
  • Women's enterprise — training, collectives and seed support

Area 04

Water & Sanitation

Clean water changes health, schooling and women's time in a single stroke — which is why it stands as its own focus area. The Trust's water programmes will begin with drinking water and basic sanitation in the communities its other programmes serve.

  • Safe drinking water access for villages and schools
  • Sanitation and hygiene — facilities plus the habits that sustain them
  • Repair and upkeep systems, so installations keep working

Area 05

Environment

Rural life runs on soil, water and tree cover — and all three are under pressure. The Trust's environment programmes will protect that base, working with the same communities on the land they depend on.

  • Tree plantation with survival care, not just planting counts
  • Restoration of ponds and local water bodies
  • Soil health and sustainable farming practices
