Updates

The record, as it happens.

Each completed step in the Trust's establishment will be recorded here — and programme announcements will follow on this page when there is work to announce.

In formation

Establishment under way

The Trust's legal establishment has begun — registration, documentation and the formalities that will let it receive and deploy funds lawfully from its first day.

In formation

Focus areas defined

Five focus areas have been set for the Trust's work: health, education, livelihoods, water & sanitation, and the environment. Programme design in each is under way.

In formation

Governance being constituted

A board of trustees is being constituted to govern the Trust. Appointments will be announced on this page, and published on the About page, as they are made.

This page will also carry the Trust's annual reports once operations begin — audited accounts, programme results and spending, published every year.
