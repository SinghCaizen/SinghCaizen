About

About the Trust.

What Singh Caizen Trusts is being established to do, how it will be governed, and the principles it will be run by.

Purpose

Why the Trust exists.

Singh Caizen Trusts is being established by the Singh Caizen group as its philanthropic arm. Its purpose is direct: to put the group's resources to work in the areas that decide a family's future — health, education, livelihoods, water and the environment.

The Trust will work where the group itself comes from — India's villages and small towns first — and grow from there as its programmes prove themselves. Nothing on this website describes work that has not begun; programmes will appear here only when they are real.

Principles

The rules the Trust sets for itself.

Need before publicity

Programmes are chosen by the need they answer, not by the attention they bring. Work that cannot be shown will still be done; work that is only for show will not.

Partnership over presence

Where a capable local organisation already works, the Trust will strengthen it rather than duplicate it. The goal is outcomes, not flags on a map.

Full accounting

Every rupee received and spent will be audited and reported. Annual reports will be published on this website from the first year of operations.

Patience

Health, education and livelihoods change slowly. The Trust will commit to places and programmes for years, and judge itself on that horizon.

Governance

Governed in the open.

The Trust will be governed by a board of trustees, constituted as part of its establishment. Appointments will be published on this page as they are made — until then, this section carries the structure, not names.

The Trust's accounts will be independently audited, and its annual report — programmes, spending and outcomes — will be published here every year.
