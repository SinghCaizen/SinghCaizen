      🛡️ Terms, Conditions, Privacy & Data Security

      1. Encryption — exactly what is and is not encrypted

      Your cloud records are end-to-end encrypted once you complete the one-time encryption setup: a random 256-bit key (AES-256-GCM, a fresh IV on every save) encrypts your transactions, watchlists, profile fields, notes, alerts and settings on your device, before anything is uploaded. That key is itself locked by your password, your 12-word vault seed phrase and your recovery codes (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations). Google Firebase then stores only ciphertext — not even Google, a database administrator, or Folio can read your portfolio. If you skip or postpone the setup, your records are instead protected by sign-in plus Firestore rules that restrict them to your own account only, and Folio will keep offering the setup. Your exported .folio vault backup is separately encrypted with AES-256-GCM and keys derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 at 250,000 iterations.

      What is not encrypted, honestly: looking up a price necessarily reveals that ticker symbol to the data source (Yahoo Finance, reached directly or through free public proxies). Your quantities, buy prices, P&L and amounts never leave your device. On-device caches hold only public price history, stored under hashed labels so your holdings and watchlist symbols are not readable in storage. The price feed is delayed ~15 minutes and is public market data.

      The trade-off, in plain words: if you lose both your password and your seed phrase (and all recovery codes), your encrypted data cannot be recovered by anyone — that is what end-to-end encryption means.

      2. Multi-Layer Account Protection (2FA & Seed Phrase)

      Optional Two-Factor Authentication (compatible with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and Authy) locks your account behind a time-based 6-digit code. It is backed by a client-side encrypted 12-word seed phrase and single-use recovery codes, plus an automatic 15-minute inactivity lock. Enable it any time in My Profile → Security.

      3. No Broker, No Bank, No Middlemen — Guaranteed

      Folio (A Singh Caizen Product) is an independent, privacy-first record-keeping and analytics terminal. It never connects to your demat account, stockbroker, or bank account, and it will never ask for your broker login credentials, MPIN, TPIN, bank account numbers, or UPI PINs. Every entry in Folio is typed in by you, manually.

      4. Zero Data Selling, Zero Ad Tracking

      Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, and Folio carries no external marketing trackers. Your personal portfolio stays 100% private to you.

      5. Your Data, Your Ownership — Export & Erasure

      You retain 100% control of your records at all times. Export your complete transaction history as a standard .csv file, or as an encrypted .folio vault backup, with one tap. You can permanently delete your cloud account and all associated records instantly from My Profile → Danger Zone, and clear all on-device data from your browser at any time.

      6. Market Data Disclaimer

      Equity quotes, index levels, historical charts, corporate actions, news feeds are aggregated from free public sources and may be delayed by approximately 15 minutes or occasionally unavailable. They are provided strictly for personal portfolio tracking and educational study — not for real-time execution.

      7. Terms of Use — No Financial or Tax Advice

      Folio — A Singh Caizen Product is a self-directed financial analytics and record-keeping software tool. Nothing displayed — including Portfolio Scores, technical indicators (RSI, EMA, SMA, Support/Resistance), tax estimates (STCG/LTCG calculations) — constitutes investment, legal or tax advice. Always consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser or a qualified chartered accountant before making investment or tax decisions.

      Welcome to Folio

      Before you begin, please take a moment to read how your information is handled here. Your trust matters to us.

      Your data is safe with us

      All your portfolio data is stored in the cloud on Google Firebase servers, under your own account only. Folio also offers one-time end-to-end encryption: after a short setup (you save a 12-word seed phrase), your records are encrypted on this device before upload, so only ciphertext is ever stored — not even Google can read them. Price lookups reveal only ticker symbols to the data source; your quantities and amounts never leave your device.

      Only you hold the keys

      Your account is protected by your password. If you turn on Two-Factor Authentication from My Profile, your secret key stays encrypted with your own password. Without your password or your seed phrase, it cannot be read — by anyone.

      No broker, no middlemen

      This website never connects to your broker or your bank, and it will never ask for your broker password, bank details, PIN or OTP.

      We do not sell your data

      Your information is used only to run this tracker for you. It is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

      Prices are delayed

      Stock prices come from free public sources and are delayed by about 15 minutes. They are suitable for tracking your portfolio — not for live trading decisions.
