      Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

      A free extra lock on your account: after your password, a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy — any). Your secret key stays encrypted with your own password — without your password or your seed phrase, nobody can read it, not even from the database. Forgotten the app? Sign in with a recovery code or your 12-word seed phrase — both work on the sign-in screen.

      Welcome to Folio

      Before you begin, please take a moment to read how your information is handled here. Your trust matters to us.

      Your data is safe with us

      All your portfolio data is stored securely in the cloud on Google Firebase servers. Your entries are saved under your own account only, and our security rules make sure that no other user can see or access your data — not even by changing a link or a setting.

      Only you hold the keys

      Your account is protected by your password. If you turn on Two-Factor Authentication from My Profile, your secret key stays encrypted with your own password. Without your password or your seed phrase, it cannot be read — by anyone.

      No broker, no middlemen

      This website never connects to your broker or your bank, and it will never ask for your broker password, bank details, PIN or OTP.

      We do not sell your data

      Your information is used only to run this tracker for you. It is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

      Prices are delayed

      Stock prices come from free public sources and are delayed by about 15 minutes. They are suitable for tracking your portfolio — not for live trading decisions.

      Not investment advice

      Folio is a personal record-keeping tool, made by Singh Caizen. Nothing on this website is investment, tax or financial advice. Please consult a SEBI-registered adviser for advice.

      Your data, your control

      You can download a CSV backup of your entries at any time from Settings, and you can permanently delete your account and all of your data from My Profile whenever you wish.

      By tapping Continue, you confirm that you have read and accepted these terms.
