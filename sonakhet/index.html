Khet se Ghar tak

Char kadam, koi bichauliya nahi

Jo kaam badi companiyan sau logo se karwati hain, hum apne ghar parivar se karte hain — isliye daam sahi, aur swaad asal.

1 · Hamare Khet

Fasal hamare apne kheton me pakti hai. Jo hum apne bachcho ko khilate hain, wahi aapke packet me jata hai.

2 · Ghar par Safai

Chalna, supa aur haath ki binnai — kankad, pathar aur toota dana alag. Phir dhoop me sukhakar nami ki jaanch.

3 · Parivar ki Packing

Har packet parivar ke haathon se bhara aur tola jata hai. Har packet par fasal ka lot number chhapa hota hai.

4 · Seedha Aapka Ghar

Order aate hi packet nikalta hai — Cash on Delivery ya UPI. Bich ke saare kharche bache, isliye gunvatta zyada, daam sahi.

Kisan ka vaada, kagaz par nahi — packet par

Hum koi badi company nahi hain jo vaade chhapti hai. Hum woh parivar hain jiski fasal aap kha rahe hain. Isliye har packet ke peeche hamara naam aur lot number khada hai.

  • Jo fasal hum apne ghar nahi khilayenge, woh packet me nahi jayegi
  • Bina polish, bina rang, bina khushboo ka chhadam
  • Har packet par lot number — khet tak ki pehchan
  • Gun me kami nikle to packet wapas, paisa wapas — bina sawal

Peedhiyon se hum kheti karte aaye hain. Fasal achhi hoti thi, par daam mandi tay karti thi — aur aapke ghar tak pahunchte-pahunchte usi fasal ka daam dugna ho jata tha.

Isiliye humne beech ka rasta hi khatm kar diya. Ab fasal hamare khet se seedha aapke ghar aati hai — saaf, taufi aur pack ki hui, parivar ke haathon se.
