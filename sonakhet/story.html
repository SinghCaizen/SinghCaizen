✦ Hamare Khet

Mitti se mohabbat,
fasal se imaan.

Ye page kisi marketing team ne nahi likha. Ye usi parivar ki baat hai jiske khet ki fasal aapke packet me aati hai.

Hum pehle kisan hain,
baad me dukaan

Hamare liye khet sirf zameen nahi hai — purkhon ki nishani hai. Bajra ho ya chana, hum wahi bote hain jo is mitti ko raas aata hai, aur wahi kaatte hain jo samay par pakta hai.

Saalon tak fasal mandi gayi. Bhav koi aur tay karta, mehnat hamari thi, munafa kisi aur ka. Ghar tak pahunchi hui wahi fasal aap dugne daam me kharidte the.

Ab hisaab seedha hai: fasal hamari, packing hamari, packet aapka. Beech wala kharcha khatm — isliye aapko asli cheez sahi daam par milti hai, aur kisan ko uski mehnat ka poora bhaav.

Bina Polish

Bazaar wali chamak tel aur rang se aati hai. Hamari daal-chana apne asli rang me milta hai — poshan poora, dikhawa zero.

Chhote Batch

Godam me saalo purana stock nahi. Fasal aati hai, saaf hoti hai, pack hoti hai — khatm hone par agle batch ka intezaar hota hai.

Haath ki Binnai

Machine ke saath-saath haath ki jaanch bhi. Kankad-pathar aur kharab dana packet me jaana to door, ghar ke janwaron ko bhi nahi jata.
