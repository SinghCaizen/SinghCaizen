About Singh Caizen

A group with one name, and one philosophy.

Singh stands for strength and leadership. Caizen — inspired by the Japanese philosophy of Kaizen, continuous improvement — is the discipline we run every business by. As a parent group, Singh Caizen provides its companies with direction, governance, capital discipline and a shared culture, while each business stays fully focused on its own customers.
