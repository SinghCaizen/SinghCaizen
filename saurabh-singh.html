Founder & Owner

Saurabh Singh

Saurabh Singh is the founder and owner of the Singh Caizen Group. He leads the group’s direction, governance and long-term investments — building every business on one conviction: that disciplined, continuous improvement can turn a small beginning into an institution.

The group he is building carries his name and his standard. Singh stands for strength and leadership; Caizen — inspired by the Japanese discipline of Kaizen — stands for getting better, every day, without exception. Together they describe how he intends every Singh Caizen business to run: proudly rooted in India, measured against the best in the world.

His way of building is deliberately unhurried. A business is announced only when its foundation is ready; a promise is made only when it can be kept. The leaders who join him will be profiled on the Leadership page as their appointments are made — until then, this page carries the standard they will be appointed to.

“We may begin in one city in India, but our standards, our ambition and our word must be global from the very first day.”
